Motion over training

How does the block-to-block motion of the 10,000 test images change as the network trains, and how fast does each site's cloud move from one checkpoint to the next?

ResNet-20 seed 0. Two clocks: the nine depth steps at each of the 40 measured checkpoints (all 40 checkpoints; SPEC figure 9), and each of the ten sites between consecutive checkpoints (all 39 training intervals measured; SPEC figure 8). Same quantities and neighbourhoods (k = 128) as the depth page.

Answer: depth clock. At initialisation (test accuracy 7.2%) every block step already moves the cloud (0.84–2.93 R² of mean squared travel), stretches neighbour distances on average (mean log-stretch +0.11 to +0.45) and leaves neighbour sets largely in place (Jaccard overlap 0.54–0.75). Over training, the stem block and the last block show the largest ranges of travel: across checkpoints the stem block moves the cloud 0.70–5.21 R² and the last block 0.44–3.68. The stem block's travel peaks at 5.21 R² at step 32 and ends at 2.64. The last block's travel falls from 2.92 at initialisation to at most 0.89 between steps 32 and 1,280 and then rises overall (3.58 at step 51,200, 3.68 at the end); its neighbour-set overlap falls, not monotonically, from 0.75 to 0.38 at step 1,280 and 0.17 at the end; and the positive tilt of its stretch profile strengthens (far minus near +0.02 at initialisation, +0.15 at the end).

Answer: training clock. Chord rates (site-mean travel divided by interval length) fall by orders of magnitude, though not monotonically: from 0.16–0.73 R per step in the first step to at most 7.58×10⁻⁶ R per step over the last interval. The chords themselves stay large for most of training: between steps 128 and 26,880 the site-mean chord per interval is 0.28–1.04 source radii, over intervals 128 to 3,840 steps long, and it is much smaller over the last interval (0.05–0.10 R between steps 51,200 and 64,000). b9 travels further than the stem, relative to its radius, in 38 of 39 intervals and has the lower neighbour-set overlap in 39; the intermediate sites are not always ordered by depth. In mid-training, site-mean straightness is almost always below 1/√2.

The depth steps at every checkpoint

Each row below is one measured checkpoint and each column one block step; the colour is one quantity of the depth page, computed exactly as there. Rows are measured checkpoints, not evenly spaced in training steps: every step from 0 to 16, then doubling to 1,024, then 1,280 and every 1,280 steps to 8,960, then sparser to 64,000. All 40 checkpoints are measured. A filmstrip is not added: the heat map already shows every checkpoint at once.

The training clock: each site between checkpoints

On this clock the two frames are the same site at consecutive measured checkpoints $t < t'$, so the displacement $v = h_l(t') - h_l(t)$ is the chord of an image's path over the interval. Because the intervals range from one training step to thousands, travel is also shown as an average chord rate, divided by $t' - t$; the toggle below rescales other quantities by the interval length too (see the caption), which makes intervals of different lengths easier to compare but does not measure what happened at the intervening steps. A chord hides reversals, so where three consecutive checkpoints exist the straightness $|x(t'') - x(t)| / (|x(t') - x(t)| + |x(t'') - x(t')|)$ is reported (1 = the two sampled chords point the same way, 0 = back where it started). The frames include the change of BatchNorm running statistics, since the network is evaluated as stored. The local fit and capture fractions are measured at 8 log-spaced intervals only (a cut made by design to stay within the budget; see the audit page); the other intervals carry items 1–9.

Straightness compares an image's net displacement over two consecutive intervals with the sum of its two chords (so it is undefined for the last interval, and it sees turning between the two chords, not within one). Over the single-step intervals at the start it is 0.70–0.98 (site means). Between steps 1,280 and 26,880 the site means are 0.37–0.73, below 1/√2 ≈ 0.71 in 119 of 120 (interval, site) cells. A single image's straightness can fall below 1/√2 only if its second chord has a component pointing back against the first; the site means show that this is common in mid-training, but they do not give the fraction of images that turn back or how much motion is undone.

Two intervals stand out. From step 16 to 32 the deepest site's cloud shrinks to 0.18 of its radius (mean log-stretch −0.45) and its mean Jaccard overlap is 0.08, against 0.65 at the stem; the stem block's depth travel peaks at the end of this interval, at step 32 (figure above). From step 33,280 to 42,240 the early sites contract instead (stem radius ratio 0.86, mean λ −0.15) while neighbour sets stay largely in place (mean Jaccard at least 0.55 at every site), and a common translation accounts for 58% of the stem's mean squared travel.

On the 8 full intervals, at rank 8, the median held-out fit score exceeds the median mean-baseline score by at most 0.18, and the mean in-sample slope share is at most 24%. Mean φv exceeds its shuffled control by at most 0.21; the largest excess is early, at b9 from step 16 to 32 (0.52 against 0.31), and the excess is smaller in the later full intervals. These are rank-8 descriptive summaries; they do not rule out other kinds of structure.

What these numbers do and do not license

  • Training-clock values are measured over intervals of very different lengths. Divided travel is an average chord rate, not an instantaneous velocity; the other rescaled quantities are interval-length normalisations only. Straightness is the only check on reversals, and only between consecutive sampled chords.
  • Depth-over-training rows are separate measurements at each checkpoint; nothing in them follows an image's neighbourhood through training.
  • Neighbourhood quantities are graph quantities at the radius of 128 neighbours. At the final checkpoint the mean distance to the 128th neighbour is at least 1.04 R in the source frames of the nine block steps, but earlier it is smaller in some frames (down to 0.38 R, b8→b9 at step 16, on the depth clock and 0.35 R on the training clock), so "local" does not mean the same at every checkpoint. At the three audited checkpoints (0, 1,280, 64,000) no sampled anchor met all gate criteria; other checkpoints were not audited. No tangent / normal or strain-tensor quantity is reported.
  • Totals are means (medians for held-out scores) over the fixed population of 10,000 test images; one seed.
