The scale audit and calibration

Does the cloud of raw activations have reproducible local planes at the scales 10,000 images give? And what do the descriptive numbers look like on synthetic clouds whose answer is known?

ResNet-20 seed 0; audit at checkpoints 0, 1,280 and 64,000 on 500 anchors (50 per class), ten sites plus the two shortcut-projected frames, k = 32 to 1,024 neighbours. Fixtures: 116 synthetic cases × 3 seeds, N = 10,000 points in R512, k = 128. SPEC.md figures 1, 11 and 10.

Answer. No anchor passed. Not one audited anchor passes the geometry gate, in any of the 864 combinations of checkpoint, frame, neighbourhood size k and plane dimension m. The neighbourhoods are not small: at the final checkpoint the mean distance to the 128th nearest neighbour is 1.02–1.25 R (audit anchors, per frame) and the mean distance to the nearest one is already 0.89–1.13 R (all images, source frames of the nine block steps), where R is the cloud's RMS distance from its own centroid. A neighbourhood of 128 images is about as wide as the cloud. The tangent / normal split of the displacement and the strain tensor (SPEC items 12–13) need a reproducible plane, so they were not computed.

On synthetic clouds, the same procedure finds the plane of a flat 8-dimensional cloud at essentially every anchor, and on a flat 32-dimensional cloud or a 64-dimensional Gaussian blob no anchor passes at any rank that k = 128 supports. The network's neighbourhood scale is like the last two. In that regime even an exactly linear map gives low slope shares and capture fractions close to a shuffled control, so those descriptive numbers do not distinguish a linear from a nonlinear motion there.

The gate, and which parts of it fail

For an anchor image, a frame, a neighbourhood size k and a plane dimension m, the geometry gate (fixed in SPEC Section 4 before any activation was looked at) asks four things of the anchor's k nearest neighbours:

  • split-half agreement: the leading m-planes of two disjoint halves of the neighbours agree, with the smallest squared cosine of their principal angles at least 0.7 (the weakest direction, not the average);
  • gap: the m-th eigenvalue of the centred local covariance is at least twice the (m+1)-th;
  • radius stability: the m-planes from the nearest k/2 and from all k neighbours agree (smallest squared cosine at least 0.7);
  • held-out secant capture: the plane fitted on one half captures at least 80% of the squared length of the other half's offsets, both when they are centred on the fitting half's mean and when they are measured from the anchor itself (the two halves swap roles and the squared lengths are pooled).

An anchor that passes all four has a reproducible leading m-plane at radius rk; that is a descriptive statement, not a claim that a manifold exists or does not.

Why: the neighbourhood is as wide as the cloud

With N points spread over deff dimensions, the distance to the k-th neighbour shrinks only like (k/N)1/deff. With tens of effective dimensions and N = 10,000, that factor is close to 1, so pair distances fall in a narrow band: in the stretch profile on the depth page the nearest neighbour is on average 0.90–1.14 R away and the farthest rank bin 1.47–1.56 R. A local plane needs a radius small against the scale on which the cloud bends or ends; at N = 10,000 none of the audited radii is.

The same numbers on clouds whose answer is known

The behaviour fixtures (SPEC Section 5A) run the full pipeline on synthetic clouds of 10,000 points in R512 at k = 128: flat planes of dimension 2, 8 and 32, anisotropic planes (half the axes at a fifth of the scale of the other half), spheres of dimension 2, 8 and 32, two intersecting sheets, and an isotropic 64-dimensional Gaussian blob, most of them both clean and with isotropic noise (the sheets and the blob clean only); and known maps on them (dilation, rotation, in-plane stretch and shear, a smooth nonlinear map, a tangential flow on the sphere, independent random displacements, shuffled endpoints, a ReLU fold). Values are means over 3 seeds. They do not gate anything; they say how to read the network's numbers.

The in-sample slope share (the part of the neighbours' relative displacement that the fitted slope explains, item 10) behaves the same way. At m = 8 an exactly linear stretch gives 75% on the flat 8-plane but 26% on the 32-plane and 17% on the blob; a nonlinear map of the 32-plane gives 24%, close to the linear one, and independent displacements 3%. The network's steps give 4%–18%. On these fixtures the slope share separated "some neighbourhood-wide trend" from "none", but not a linear from a nonlinear motion.

Exact checks, and what the run cost

The 16 tests in tests/test_core.py (run 2026-11-24 in float64 on small clouds: all 16 pass with DADApy installed; without it the DADApy comparison is skipped) cover SPEC Section 5A's exact cases. A translation and rotations by 0.1, 1 and 3 rad leave every edge length unchanged, with q = 0 for the translation and q > 0 for the rotations; a planar rotation of a planar cloud gives the radial-fit coefficient cos θ − 1; uniform dilation by c gives log-stretch log c and Green strain (c² − 1)/2 on every edge; the split of mean squared travel and the budget of the local fit close; log-stretch adds over a projection and a following map, and Green strain composes as eS + eB + 2 eS eB; unresolved and exactly collapsed edges are counted separately (and left out of the summaries they would make undefined); at three sample anchors the batched computation reproduces a direct computation of the edge, fit, capture and gate fields; a linear map of a 4-plane is captured fully at rank 4 and motion normal to the plane not at all; the geometry gate passes on more than 95% of anchors on a flat plane and on fewer than 5% on a blob; float32 work matches float64 on five fields to a relative tolerance of 10−3 (absolute 10−4); straightness is 1 on a straight path and 0 on a return; the stretch profile survives degenerate pairs; the low-rank predictor skips ranks the fitting half does not span; and the Gride estimate matches DADApy to a relative 2×10−3 at six scales and lies within 0.6 of the true dimension at two of them.

Selected cross-checks in the full-size runs agree. On the fixtures, the pooled means give: dilation reproduces log c to within 2.78×10⁻¹⁷; rotations give |mean log-stretch| and mean edge strain energy below 1.66×10⁻¹⁶ with mean q at least 0.048. On a 2,000-point, 4,096-dimensional fixture the batched and direct computations of q and φs agree to 3.78×10⁻¹⁵ at three sample anchors per run. On the network at the final checkpoint, the stage-change identities hold to 2.23×10⁻¹⁶. The candidate-based neighbour search matched a direct float64 search on 200 anchors at the stem and at b7.

Cost: the run's ledger (data/euclid/_ledger.json) estimates 29.5 USD in all, against the 30 USD ceiling; Modal's billing report (2026-11-25) shows about 24 USD actually billed. The ledger is deliberately conservative: each of the 117 worker units is charged its measured run time plus a fixed startup and idle allowance, a failed unit is charged its full timeout, and each launch's coordinator is charged its wall time plus startup. By the ledger, the fixtures and the audit alone took 10.5 USD, more than projected, so three pieces were cut by design (SPEC Section 7): the training clock carries the local fit and capture fractions at 8 log-spaced intervals only, the neighbourhood-size check is k = 32 against 128 only (no k = 512), and the 5,000-image thinning is run at k = 128 only (no matched-radius run at k = 64). All 10 recorded launches have finished.

Sensitivity at the final checkpoint

Weighting changes little: Gaussian weights change mean λ by at most 0.001 and e² and q by at most 0.6%, consistent with neighbour distances that are similar on average (the ratio of the mean nearest-neighbour distance to the mean 128th is 0.85–0.92), which makes the weights close to uniform. Neighbourhood size matters little: with k = 32 the radius is still 0.98–1.21 R, mean λ moves by at most 0.03, and e² and q by at most 15% of their value, and the order of the steps by travel, λ, e² and q is unchanged. The metric matters at early steps; the last block changes less: with every channel rescaled to unit RMS, b2→b3's small mean λ changes sign (−0.025 raw, +0.031 rescaled) and b1→b2's mean squared travel rises from 0.69 to 0.95 R² (the edge strain energies of b1→b2 and b5→b6, both small, change by factors of 1.9 and 2.8); the last block changes less (travel 3.52 R², mean λ +0.59, Jaccard 0.17). Sample size: across five subsets of 5,000 images at k = 128, the range (highest minus lowest subset) of travel and q is at most 1.1% of the full-population value and the range of mean λ at most 0.005, but halving N widens the neighbourhood (r128/R 1.07–1.27) and their average shifts mean λ by up to 0.014 from the full-population value. A second seed agrees at the end of the network, not at its start: seed 1's last block moves the cloud 3.56 R² (seed 0: 3.68), has a mean log-stretch of +0.60 with a similar positive tilt across scales (+0.16, seed 0 +0.15) and a similarly low neighbour-set overlap (Jaccard 0.17; seed 0: 0.17); its two stage-change blocks move the cloud 1.20–1.82 R². But its stem block moves the cloud 0.95 R² with a mean λ of +0.35 (seed 0: 2.64 and +0.64), and its b2→b3 has a positive mean log-stretch (+0.14) where seed 0's shows a slight mean contraction (−0.03). These comparisons are at the final checkpoint only. A k = 512 run and a matched-radius thinning at k = 64 were dropped by design to stay under the cost ceiling.

What this licenses

  • Every neighbourhood quantity on these pages is a graph quantity at a radius comparable to the cloud's own radius at the final checkpoint: "local" there means "at the scale of the whole cloud". (Early in training the radius is smaller in some frames; see the training page.)
  • No tangent or normal component, strain tensor, plane rotation or second-order quantity is reported, because no reproducible local plane was found to define them on. This is a statement about what 10,000 points resolve, not a claim that the activations lie on no manifold.
  • Capture fractions and fit shares are descriptive. In this regime a value near the shuffled control or a low slope share is what an exactly linear map also gives, so neither is evidence of nonlinearity.
